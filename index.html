Technical Excellence

Our 4-Step Cleaning Method

We follow a rigorous process to ensure complete soil extraction without oversaturating inner foam layers.

01

1. Dry Vacuuming

High-suction dry extraction removes hair, dust, and particulate matter before applying moisture.

02

2. Pre-Spray & Agitate

Eco-friendly enzyme solutions break down bodily oils and food stains using soft agitation brushes.

03

3. 100°C Steam Blast

Pressurized thermal steam sanitizes deep cushion fibers, killing dust mites, bedbugs, and bacteria.

04

4. Wet Extraction

High-lift vacuum motor extracts dirty water and moisture, leaving fabric dry in roughly 2 hours.
